# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead / Quality Assurance Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads own quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance criteria validation. They collaborate with product and engineering teams to ensure deliverables meet defined quality standards and user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality standards and testing strategy
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Lead QA team in executing manual and automated testing
- Identify quality risks and blockers early
- Validate acceptance criteria before sign-off
- Coordinate with developers on test automation and CI integration
- Report quality metrics and trends to PM and Project Manager

### Goals
- Ensure all features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Reduce production defects and customer-reported issues
- Enable fast, confident releases with clear quality gates

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- Test plan reviews and acceptance criteria refinement
- Quality metrics reporting and risk escalation
- Post-release defect triage and incident response

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on test automation, edge cases, and regression testing
- **Product Manager**: Refine acceptance criteria and prioritize quality initiatives
- **Project Manager**: Flag quality risks and dependencies affecting timelines
- **Release Manager**: Execute go/no-go decisions based on quality gates

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction and guidance for feature design, implementation, and system architecture. They mentor developers, review technical designs, and identify technical risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical design proposals and architecture decisions
- Mentor developers on best practices, patterns, and standards
- Identify technical risks and dependencies
- Lead technical design reviews and spike investigations
- Ensure code quality and maintainability standards
- Support release planning from a technical readiness perspective

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and well-architected solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Accelerate developer productivity through mentorship and clarity

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture discussions
- Code review and technical feedback
- Technical risk escalation
- Planning and estimation collaboration

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical guidance, mentor on implementation
- **Project Manager**: Flag technical risks and dependencies
- **Product Manager**: Advise on technical trade-offs and feasibility
- **QA Lead**: Collaborate on test strategy for complex technical components

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and coach the organization on agile practices. They enable the team to work efficiently and continuously improve their delivery process.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and blockers preventing team progress
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Protect team focus and manage sprint scope
- Track and report on team velocity and process metrics
- Mentor team on continuous improvement and self-organization

### Goals
- Enable high-performing, self-organizing teams
- Increase delivery velocity and consistency
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and ceremony facilitation
- One-on-ones with team members for coaching
- Retrospective action item tracking
- Agile metrics and process improvement discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate on timeline and deliverable tracking
- **Developers**: Coach on agile practices, remove blockers
- **Product Manager**: Clarify product priorities and backlog refinement
- **All Roles**: Foster psychological safety and team collaboration

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve project scope, and ensure alignment with organizational goals and strategy. They are decision-makers who ensure the project delivers value to the business.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Approve project charter and budget
- Make priority and trade-off decisions
- Ensure stakeholder alignment and buy-in
- Remove organizational blockers and escalations
- Monitor project health and ROI

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment across the organization
- Minimize business risk and maximize ROI

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and charter approval
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Escalation and decision-making forums
- Stakeholder briefings and announcements

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Receive status updates, make decisions on scope and timeline
- **Product Manager**: Validate business outcomes and success metrics
- **Development Team**: Understand business context and priorities

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads collaborate on user experience, design specifications, and usability validation. They ensure features are designed with end users in mind and meet accessibility and usability standards.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user personas
- Create design specifications and wireframes
- Collaborate with product and development on feasibility and implementation
- Validate designs with end users and conduct usability testing
- Ensure accessibility standards (WCAG, etc.) are met
- Maintain design systems and consistency across products

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and usable features
- Reduce user friction and improve adoption
- Build empathy for end users across the team

### Typical Communication
- Design review and critique sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Usability test results and recommendations
- Design handoff and implementation guidance

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Align on user needs and feature requirements
- **Developers**: Collaborate on design feasibility and implementation
- **QA Lead**: Validate usability and accessibility in testing
- **Stakeholders**: Present user research and design rationale

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployments, manage release calendars, and ensure smooth handoffs to production. They work across teams to plan, execute, and verify releases while minimizing risk and downtime.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain release calendars and deployment windows
- Coordinate cross-team release planning and dependencies
- Execute release checklists and pre-deployment verification
- Manage rollback and incident response procedures
- Communicate release status to stakeholders and support teams
- Document release notes and deployment procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and safe releases to production
- Minimize deployment risks and customer impact
- Ensure clear communication and coordination across teams

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and dependency coordination
- Pre-deployment verification and go/no-go decisions
- Release notes and stakeholder announcements
- Post-deployment verification and incident response

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate on release timeline and dependencies
- **QA Lead**: Validate quality gates and go/no-go criteria
- **Developers**: Coordinate on deployment procedures and rollback plans
- **Technical Lead**: Resolve technical readiness and deployment issues
- **Stakeholders**: Communicate release status and timeline
